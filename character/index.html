How to Use

Tabs: Combat for gameplay stats, Cards for abilities, Inventory for items, Story for narrative.
Saving: All changes auto-save to your browser. Use Export to download a backup JSON file. Use Import to restore from a file. Sign in to sync to the cloud.
Cards & Abilities: Tap + Add to browse the SRD library. Click any card on your sheet to read it in full. Star up to 5 domain cards for your active loadout.
Gear: Two weapon slots and one armor slot are fixed. Use + Gear to add shields, rings, cloaks, etc.
Theme: Pick an accent color with the color swatch. Click the mode button to cycle between Dark, Light, Sci-Fi, and Fantasy themes.
