Homebrew Guidelines

⚔️ Domain Cards
  • Ability — a non-magical action or passive benefit. Usually costs Hope or has a "once per rest" limit.
  • Spell — a magical effect. Requires a Spellcast Roll and costs a spell slot or has a Recall Cost.
  • Recall Cost — how many slots it takes to prepare. Higher level cards typically cost more (Lv 1–3 = 1, Lv 4–6 = 2, Lv 7+ = 3).
  • Level — when the card becomes available. Match the power level to the tier (Lv 1–2 = Tier 1, Lv 3–4 = Tier 2, etc.).
  • Keep effects focused — one clear thing per card. If it does too much, split it into two cards.
🧬 Ancestries
  • SRD pattern: 2 features — one passive (always on) and one active (triggered or limited use).
  • Features should be flavorful and broadly useful, not combat-only.
🏠 Communities
  • SRD pattern: 1 feature — usually grants advantage on a specific type of social or knowledge roll.
  • Think about what growing up in this community teaches you.
📜 Classes & Subclasses
  • Classes have a Hope Feature (costs Hope to activate) and Class Features (core mechanics).
  • Subclasses have 3 tiers: Foundation (Lv 1), Specialization (Lv 5), Mastery (Lv 9). Power scales with tier.
💡 General Tips
  • Don't duplicate existing SRD cards — check the Compendium first.
  • Write clear, concise feature text. If a player can't understand it in 10 seconds, simplify.
  • Playtest before publishing when possible. Theory and table experience are different.
  • Use the description field to explain your design intent — it helps others decide if it fits their table.
