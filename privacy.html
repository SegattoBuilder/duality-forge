Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Overview

Duality Forge is a free, fan-made tabletop RPG toolkit. Your privacy matters — we collect only what's needed to make the tool work and we never sell your data.

2. Data We Collect

Account & Profile (optional — only if you sign in)

  • Email address and authentication credentials (managed by Supabase)
  • Google profile info if you use Google sign-in (name, profile picture, email)
  • Profile details you provide: nickname, avatar URL, country, state/region, DM experience level

Game Data (stored when you use the tools)

  • Character sheet: name, pronouns, level, heritage, class, stats, HP/stress/hope/armor, thresholds, description fields, backstory, connections, level-up notes
  • Cards, domain selections, gear, weapons, armor, inventory items, consumables, gold, experience entries
  • DM tools: campaign name, combat tracker creatures, action counters, fear pool, vault creatures/groups, chronicle entries (chapters, NPCs, music cues)

Feedback & Error Reports

  • Bug reports and feature requests you submit (message text, page URL, report type)
  • Automatic JavaScript error logs (error message, source file, line/column, page URL, browser user agent)
  • Feedback reports are stored in Cloudflare KV and automatically expire after 30 days

Analytics

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: privacy-friendly page view analytics — no cookies, no personal data, no tracking across sites

Local Storage

  • Your game data is auto-cached in your browser's localStorage for offline access
  • Theme and display mode preferences are stored locally
  • This data never leaves your device unless you sign in for cloud sync

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To save and sync your game data across devices when signed in
  • To display your profile (nickname, avatar) within the app
  • To fix bugs and improve the tool using error reports and feedback
  • To understand basic page usage via privacy-friendly analytics

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Supabase — authentication, user profiles, and cloud-synced game data
  • Cloudflare Pages & KV — site hosting and temporary feedback/error report storage
  • Your browser — localStorage for auto-cached game data and preferences

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is only processed by the infrastructure providers listed above (Supabase, Cloudflare) as necessary to operate the service.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Game data is kept as long as your account exists
  • Feedback and error reports auto-expire after 30 days
  • You can delete your cloud saves at any time from within the app
  • To request full account and data deletion, contact us via the feedback form
  • Local data can be cleared by clearing your browser storage

7. Cookies

Duality Forge does not use tracking cookies. Supabase may use essential cookies/tokens for authentication sessions. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookie-free.

8. Children

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us to have it removed.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
