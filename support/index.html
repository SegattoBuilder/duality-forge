How to Use

🗡️ Character Sheet
▶
Tabs: Combat for gameplay stats, Cards for abilities, Inventory for items, Story for narrative.
Saving: All changes auto-save to your browser. Use the ⋮ menu to export/import a backup JSON file. Sign in to sync to the cloud.
Cards & Abilities: Tap + Add to browse the SRD library. Click any card on your sheet to read it in full. Star up to 5 domain cards for your active loadout.
Gear: Two weapon slots and one armor slot are fixed. Use + Gear to add shields, rings, cloaks, etc.
Theme: Open the ⋮ menu to pick an accent color or switch between Dark, Light, Sci-Fi, and Fantasy display modes.
⚔️ DM Tools
▶
Tracker: Add adversaries, characters, or custom creatures. Track HP, stress, hope, armor with dot trackers. Drag & drop to reorder.
Vault: Stash creatures between encounters using the 📦 button. Pull them back to the tracker when needed.
Chronicle: Session notes organized by chapters. Add NPCs, music cues, and key events.
Adversaries: Search the full SRD adversary database. Filter by tier, difficulty, and type.
Compendium: Browse weapons, armors, items, classes, domain cards, and more from the SRD.
Saving: All changes auto-save to your browser. Use the ⋮ menu to save/load a JSON file. Sign in to sync to the cloud.
