How to Use

🗡️ Character Sheet
▶
Tabs: Combat for stats & gear, Cards for abilities, Inventory for items & gold, Story for backstory & notes, Compendium to browse all game data.
Vitals: Click dots to fill/unfill HP, Stress, Hope, and Armor. Use the +/− buttons next to each tracker to adjust the max. Damage thresholds auto-calculate from your equipped armor + level + extras.
Gear: Add multiple weapons, armors, items, and consumables. Star (★) a weapon or armor to equip it — this feeds into your Attack Bonus and Damage Thresholds automatically. Collapse items with ▼ to keep things tidy.
Cards & Abilities: Tap + Add to browse the card library — ancestries, communities, classes, subclasses, and domain cards. Click any card on your sheet to read it in full. Star (★) up to 5 domain cards as your active loadout — selected cards auto-sort to the top by level. Non-selected cards are grayed out.
Compendium: Browse weapons, armors, items, consumables, classes, subclasses, ancestries, communities, domain cards, and rules. Filter by category and search by name. Click any entry to see full details — then hit the "Add" button to send it straight to your character sheet (weapons go to Weapons, armors to Armor, domain cards to your Cards tab, etc.).
Inventory: Track gold (handfuls & bags) and add custom inventory items with quantities.
Story: Character description fields (clothes, eyes, body, skin, attitude), backstory, connections, and level-up notes — all free-text.
Saving: All changes auto-save to your browser. Use the ⚙️ menu to export/import a backup JSON file, or start a new character. Sign in to sync to the cloud — auto-saves every 5 minutes.
Theme: Open the ⚙️ menu to pick from 10 accent colors and switch between Dark, Light, Sci-Fi, and Fantasy display modes.
⚔️ DM Tools
▶
Tracker: Add characters, enemies (from the database), or fully custom creatures. Track HP, stress, hope, and armor with dot trackers — click dots to fill/unfill, use +/− to adjust max. Add action counters to track rounds, lair actions, or anything else. Drag & drop cards to reorder. Use 📝 to flip any card and write notes on the back. Use ✏️ to edit a creature's stats at any time.
Fear Pool: 12-dot fear tracker at the top of the Tracker tab. Click dots to fill as fear builds. Use the 🗑 Fear button to reset it between encounters.
Vault: Stash creatures between encounters using the 📦 button on any tracker card. Create groups to organize creatures by encounter or scene — name them, mark as disposable (auto-deletes the group after deploying), and drag & drop creatures between groups. Deploy a single creature or an entire group back to the tracker with one click. Choose "Deploy" to reset HP/stress to full, or "As-Is" to keep current values. Collapse/expand groups to keep things organized.
Chronicle: Session notes organized by chapters. Each chapter has a free-text area for notes, plus dedicated sections for NPCs (name, faction, disposition, notes) and music cues (scene label + link or text — links become clickable 🔗). Drag & drop chapters to reorder. Collapse chapters you're not using.
Adversaries: Search the full adversary database by name. Filter by tier, difficulty range, and creature type. Each result shows full stats, attacks, features, and abilities. Hit "+ Add" on any adversary to instantly add it to your tracker with all stats pre-filled.
Compendium: Browse weapons, armors, items, consumables, classes, subclasses, ancestries, communities, domain cards, and rules. Filter by category and search by name.
Saving: All changes auto-save to your browser. Use the ⚙️ menu to save a backup JSON file or start a new campaign. Sign in to sync to the cloud — auto-saves every 5 minutes. Load any of your cloud saves from the ☁️ menu.
